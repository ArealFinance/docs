## Real World Engine

Areal assembles a basket of income-generating real-world assets from around the world: cars for
rental fleets, capsule hotels, renewable-energy (solar and wind) stations, and more. Each asset
is tokenized and added to one shared basket that earns real, off-chain yield.

The protocol and its basket are governed by the community, which decides which assets to acquire
and how the income they generate is distributed. The engine turns scattered, illiquid real-world
cash flows into a single liquid, on-chain, community-governed portfolio.

The result is a **productive store of value**: instead of sitting idle, capital is held in real
assets that earn, so value is both preserved and grown from real-world yield.

## Real World Token (RWT)

**RWT is the protocol's utility token.** It reflects your contribution to Areal's RWA basket and
to the growth of the real-world assets within it. Holding RWT means taking part in funding the
basket and sharing in the value those assets build over time.

## Staked RWT (stRWT)

**stRWT is the staked version of RWT.** Stake your RWT to receive stRWT and earn a share of
protocol income, paid as a steadily rising stRWT to RWT exchange rate. Rewards compound
automatically, with no claiming.

## Core Principles

- **Risk management.** The basket is diversified across asset types and regions, with conservative reserves and clear risk disclosure.
- **Transparency.** Every asset, balance, and metric is on-chain and verifiable. Book NAV is RWT's honest, fundamental price.
- **Community governance.** The protocol and its basket are owned and steered by the community, which decides asset acquisition and income distribution.
- **Capital efficiency.** Income is continuously reinvested and routed to its most productive use, so capital compounds instead of sitting idle.
- **User convenience.** A simple experience: hold RWT for passive real-world yield or stake it for more, with no deep DeFi knowledge required.
