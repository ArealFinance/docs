## How reporting works

- **Per asset.** Each asset in the basket reports its gross income, servicing costs, and net
  income for the period, along with its yield relative to the asset's value.
- **Into the basket.** Net income is added to the basket on-chain, raising Book NAV, and split by
  the [distribution model](/how-it-works/overview).
- **Verifiable.** Reported figures reconcile with the on-chain change in invested capital and Book
  NAV, so the numbers can always be checked against the chain.

See [Rental Cars](/revenue/reports/rental-cars) and the other Reports pages for the per-asset breakdown.
